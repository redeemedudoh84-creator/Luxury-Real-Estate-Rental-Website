Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

You can find a detailed breakdown of our villa and service prices on our "Villas & Rates" page. Alternatively, you can download our latest digital brochure or contact our sales team directly for a personalized quote based on your preferred dates and requirements.

The accommodation price typically includes the full rental of the villa, standard utilities (water, electricity, high-speed Wi-Fi), regular housekeeping services, and full access to all on-site amenities such as the pool, gym, and recreational areas. Additional services—such as private chefs, airport transfers, and personalized excursions—are not included but can be arranged for an additional fee.

Booking with us is simple:

  1. Submit an inquiry through our website or contact our booking team.
  2. Our team will confirm availability and provide a formal rental agreement.
  3. To secure your reservation, you must sign the agreement and pay the required advance payment.

Once these steps are completed, you will receive a booking confirmation and a welcome packet.

We offer flexible payment options. Typically, a 30% advance payment is required to secure the booking. The remaining balance can either be paid in full 30 days prior to your arrival, or you can choose to pay in several installments. Please discuss your preferred payment plan with your booking agent during the reservation process.

If we do not receive your advance payment or remaining balance by the due date, our team will issue a reminder. We offer a short grace period of [X] days. If payment is not received within this period, a late fee of [Y]% may be applied. In cases of severe delays, we reserve the right to cancel the reservation and apply our standard cancellation policy.

Cancellations must be submitted in writing to our support team. Our refund policy is tiered based on the timing of the cancellation:

  • More than 60 days before arrival: Full refund minus a small administrative fee.
  • 30 to 60 days before arrival: The advance payment is forfeited, but any remaining payments are refunded.
  • Less than 30 days before arrival: The reservation is non-refundable.

We highly recommend purchasing travel insurance to protect against unforeseen circumstances.
